The photographs and painting are supplied brand-reference imagery. Their presence does not establish a real TANORA event, location, performer, date, or historical chronology. Website captions should describe what is visible and must not invent those details.

## Fonts

Source archive: `D:/tanora/franklin-gothic.zip`.

- `src/fonts/FranklinGothic.ttf`: exact `FranklinGothic.ttf` entry; internal family Franklin Gothic, style Regular, PostScript name FranklinGothic. It is visually broad and heavy despite the Regular label.
- `src/fonts/FranklinGothicCondensed.ttf`: exact `Franklin Gothic Condensed.ttf` entry; internal family Franklin Gothic, style Condensed, PostScript name FranklinGothicCondensed.
- The archive's `FRANKGO.ttf` is byte-identical to `Franklin Gothic Condensed.ttf`; only one copy is included.
- The two italic entries are also duplicates and are not needed for the initial site.

The source ZIP has five entries and three unique font files. No typeface substitution, synthetic font conversion, or glyph changes were performed.

## Composition notes

The footer and wordmark contain transparent padding. Scale and position based on visible bounds without painting a white background. The footer's gold emblem intentionally extends past its bottom canvas edge.

The hero background is generated separately and is documented by its authoring agent; it is not part of this copied-asset manifest.
